SWAMP BATTLE

Hide five things in the swamp, then take turns firing at each other's. Every message you send is one turn with one shot. Take out everything they hid to win.

YOUR GAMES

    HOW IT WORKS

    10 × 10 swamp. Each side hides a stilt hut (5), boardwalk (4), raft (3), canoe (3) and log (2). Fire one shot, send the link, and your friend's game answers it. Play each game from the same browser you started it in, since that's where your hiding spots are kept.

    SAM CHALLENGES YOU

    Their first shot is already on its way. Hide your five pieces, see where it landed, then fire back.

    HIDE YOUR PIECES

    Tap a piece to pick it, then tap the water to move it there. Rotate turns the picked piece.
